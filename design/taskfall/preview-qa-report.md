# Taskfall · Preview / QA Report

## Prototype coverage

The implementation target covers Home, Capture, Sorting, Pause, Result, and Edit. The preview is intentionally a single-file validation aid and does not claim device comfort validation.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Paste 20 Chinese tasks, start, and sort all four ways.
2. Leave at least one card untouched and verify it appears in Undecided.
3. Re-grab a card from a basket and move it.
4. Enter Pause, Reset, and Exit.
5. Confirm >26-character text is exactly two visual lines with ellipsis.
6. Complete a session and export the result view as a screenshot.

## Open validation

Measure completion time, misdrop rate, perceived comfort, and controller parity on Project Swan / PICO OS 6.
