# Taskfall · Research Report

## Domain observations

Todo triage is a rapid prioritization task with low tolerance for friction. The main risks are decision fatigue, accidental loss, and overloading the visual field. A spatial presentation is useful for motion, proximity, and tangible grouping, but not for dense text entry or long-term archive browsing.

## Adjacent benchmark

Todoist / Microsoft To Do: strong capture and list editing; avoid list-only anxiety and deep navigation. Trello: strong visual grouping; avoid board density and drag distances. Paper sticky notes: strong tangible manipulation and recoverability; avoid physical clutter and lack of persistence. These are task-level observations only; Taskfall derives its own layout and state graph.

## Differentiation opportunity

Use the spatial field for a bounded, playful triage moment, then return to a calm, explicit result list. The “Undecided” pool is a trust feature, not a failure state. Color, icon, label, and sound all redundantly identify each destination.

## Evidence gaps

No user study or device comfort measurement was supplied. MVP validation should measure completion time, misdrop rate, and whether users understand the four destinations without tutorial repetition.
